I am a Ph.D. Candidate at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA). Previously, I received my bachelor's degree from Wuhan University. My research lies at the intersection of Computational Psychology, Test-Time Reasoning, and LLM Scientific Reasoning—building interpretable, principled methods to understand and enhance how large language models reason across social, cognitive, and physical domains.

我目前是中国科学院自动化研究所（CASIA）的在读博士研究生，本科毕业于武汉大学。我的研究聚焦于计算心理学、Test-Time 推理优化与大语言模型科学推理，致力于通过可解释且具理论基础的方法，理解并增强大语言模型在社会认知与物理学等复杂领域中的逻辑与计算推理能力。

Research Focus研究方向

Computational Psychology计算心理学

Cognitive and social psychology modeling for LLMs, including theory-guided probing of social attribution, cognitive emotion structures, and belief-constrained strategic interaction.

面向大语言模型的认知心理学与社会心理学建模，涵盖社会归因机制探测、情绪认知结构统一建模及信念约束下的策略对话。

Test-Time ReasoningTest-Time 推理优化

Optimizing test-time reasoning in latent space to improve robustness, interpretability, and scaling efficiency—moving beyond token-level sampling via direct credit assignment.

探索大语言模型在测试阶段的推理优化、可解释性与能力增强，通过潜空间自适应与梯度信用分配突破表层采样瓶颈。

LLM Scientific ReasoningLLM 科学推理

Enhancing logical faithfulness and computational rigor in physics reasoning through scientific logicality evaluation, logic-guided sampling, and execution-grounded formula synthesis.

以物理学为代表，研究基于大语言模型的科学逻辑性评估、逻辑增强训练与可执行公式实例化的数据合成方法。
